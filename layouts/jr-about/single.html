Emotions. [and] Baking. That's why you're here ...
Emotions. There are lots of them, and they vary, but each one is important. Don't ignore them, they're telling you something – even if it's hard to hear.
Here, at Emotional Baking jr., you'll see that you can tackle any problem, big or small when you break it down into little steps. You'll see you can follow a recipe step-by-step, work through your feelings, and enjoy the treat you made. You can do it, but you don't have to do it alone - call a friend, a family member, or someone you want to share with.
So, if you're feeling (like baking) ...
something that fits with the mood you're in,
something that helps manage your feelings,
something that's distracting and/or fun.
something that tastes good ...
You've come to the right place.
