  ingredients:
  - ¼ cup + 2 tbsp (30 g) unsweetened desiccated coconut, toasted
  - 2 cups (236 g) apple, diced (approx. 2 medium apples)
  - ¼ cup + 2 tbsp (30 g) brown sugar (or 2 tbsp caramel sauce)
  - 1/8 tsp of kosher salt
  - 2 cups (304 g) all purpose flour
  - ¼ tsp salt
  - 4 tsp baking powder
  - ¾ cup (166 g) sugar
  - ¾ cup vegetable oil
  - 2 large eggs
  - ¼ cup + 2 tbsp maple syrup
  - ½ tsp vanilla extract
