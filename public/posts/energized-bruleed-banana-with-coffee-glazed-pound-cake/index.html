Energized bruleed banana with coffee-glazed pound cake
The heady mix of feeling energized AND motivated at the same time is as rare as it is special - it’s electric. It’s hard to sit still when the mood is this exciting. There’s an urge to take advantage of the moment because it feels intriguing but also fleeting. It’s time to seize this energized mood and move...
And once you get moving - things will fall into place. You might find yourself in an even better position than when you started. Keep the energy going with a fun and special treat. Relish this energized mood (and bake)!
Well, not bake, brulee. Brulee a banana. A banana coated in sugar and caramelized to perfection.

bruleed banana with coffee-glazed pound cake
00:15
00:10
00:05
Prep time: 10 minutes, Bake time: 2-5 minutes
Makes 2 servings
Ingredients
  • 1 Banana, cut in half, lengthwise
  • ¾ tbsp granulated sugar
  • 2 slices of pound cake
  • ½ tsp instant coffee powder
  • ½ tsp granulated sugar
  • ¾ tsp hot water
  • 2 tbsp walnuts
Instructions
Keep the excitement going...
Sprinkle the sugar over the bananas.
Set the oven to broil on high. Bake the bananas for 2-3 minutes, until the sugar caramelizes. *Keep an eye that it doesn’t burn.
It smells so good already!
In a separate bowl, add the sugar and coffee powder. Stir in the hot water. Set aside.
Brush the coffee mixture over the pound cake.
Okay, yummm!!
To a plate, add the bruleed banana, the glazed pound cake, and the walnuts.
Making this dessert is the best way to indulge in the energized mood. It’s fun, it’s exciting, and it’s a delightful way to not ‘sit still’.
