It’s the season of togetherness. Whether it’s boisterous celebrations, or quiet nights in with friends and family - the season brings with it warmth and nostalgia.
This holiday calls for a mood playlist that’s happy, cozy, and bright. It’s a delicious complement to the season and the mood.
Thoughtful hot chocolate affogato.
Lighthearted spiced plum tart.
Nostalgic OG Chocolate Chip Cookies.
Merry honey lime cake.
It’s Hot Chocolate Affogato to be enjoyed with family and friends, or on a thoughtful evening by the fire, solo.
It’s Spiced Plum Tarts and cheerful music, a few nostalgic and familiar sights, togetherness, and laughter.
It’s Chocolate Chip Cookies and making new memories.
It’s Honey Lime Loaf Cake and friends, family, and loved ones savouring the small moments.

Playful and free
A playful mood is carefree, simple, and fun.
It’s fun because it’s easy. In a playful mood nothing’s taken too seriously - there’s room to be easygoing and lighthearted; perfectly free to live in the mood.
It’s possible to enjoy the mood even more with the help of this playlist. It’s filled with all things fun and then some. Linger in the fun and bake your way through this playful mood...

Bored. And Moving On...
Bored but not for long. Step away from the dull, annoying, frustrating mood and step toward something fun, something interesting. Call a friend, go out for a walk, go somewhere new, or bake something fun. Baking is a sure-fire way to shift away from the mood and mix things up.
Don’t settle for boredom, explore the possibilities. Use this playlist as a reference on your way toward adventure. Move on from feeling bored and bake!

Rainy Afternoon
Rainy afternoons - they’re made for baking. Leave the cold, wet, uncomfortable weather outside and stay in, under a warm blanket, with a hot cup of tea. Thoughts of freshly baked cookies, honeyed fruit salad, warm buttered bread, and spiced affogato come to mind. It seems like the perfect opportunity to bake, relax, bake some more, and take some time to enjoy the rainy day.
This Mood Playlist is about just that – it’s about the warmth of baking...

Gloomy pound cake fruit salad.
Dreary sour crusty bread.
Cozy spiced pumpkin tea affogato
It’s Maple Oatmeal Cookies and that warm and toasty feeling.
It’s Pound Cake Fruit Salad and a peaceful way to enjoy the mood.
It’s Sour Crusty Bread and an enjoyable task on a bleak day.
It’s Spiced Ginger Pumpkin Affogato and that warm and cozy feeling that fills you up from the inside out...
